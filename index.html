The Redneck EdTech Philosophy

"Teaching is expensive. I am tired of people charging obscene prices for software that almost fits your class or charges monthly memberships just so you can update your curriculum and reach your kids today. That has to stop."

All materials on this site are offered free to use in an as-is condition. However, if there is something you genuinely want or need for your classroom and I don't have it yet, email me at harl.roehm@redneckedtech.com and I will try to create it for you.

Please Keep In Mind: I am a father, teacher, presenter, and keynote speaker. Please be patient. As a solo individual creating this content, requests may take a little time to create, but have faith. If I give you my word that I will build it, you can bank on the fact that I will do my absolute best to help you out in the classroom!
